# Lyric audit: four newly source-backed compositions

## Promises
Primary: faithfulness, trust in god. Secondary: covenant promises, perseverance.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/promises-maverick-city/
Notes: Repeated celebration of divine faithfulness and trusting God through hardship. Hebrews 6:13–20 appears on the publisher page.

## The Passion
Primary: atonement, freedom from sin. Secondary: resurrection, love of christ.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/the-passion-hillsong-worship/
Notes: Cross, substitutionary death, forgiveness and liberation are central. Resurrection is affirmed in the bridge but not tagged primary.

## Made for More
Primary: identity in christ, new life. Secondary: grace, calling.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/made-for-more-josh-baldwin/
Notes: Lyrics emphasize belonging to Christ, forgiveness and a transformed life; grave imagery primarily describes spiritual renewal.

## The Lion and the Lamb
Primary: kingship of christ, christ as lamb. Secondary: atonement, deliverance.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/lion-and-the-lamb/
Notes: Contrasts conquering Lion with sacrificial Lamb. Publisher cites Philippians 2:10 and Isaiah 14:27.
