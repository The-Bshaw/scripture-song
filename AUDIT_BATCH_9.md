# Audit batch 9 — song-specific published evidence

## The Stand
Primary: surrender, devotion to god. Secondary: atonement, salvation, holy spirit.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/the-stand-united/
Note: The song emphasizes responding to God with the whole life; cross and salvation frame the response. Scripture leads: Romans 12:1 and Galatians 2:20 (editorial).

## Cornerstone
Primary: trust in christ, assurance. Secondary: righteousness in christ, second coming.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/cornerstone-hillsong-worship/
Note: Confidence in Christ as the cornerstone through hardship; publisher cites Isaiah 28:16 and Psalm 118:21–23.

## Resurrender
Primary: surrender, renewal of the church. Secondary: obedience, unity.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/resurrender-hillsong-worship/
Note: The communal call to yield again to God and restoration of his people is central; publisher lists 1 Corinthians 3:16 and 1 Corinthians 1:30.

## Fairest Lord Jesus
Primary: beauty of christ, adoration. Secondary: creation, kingship of christ.
Source: https://hymnary.org/text/fairest_lord_jesus_ruler_of_all_nature
Note: Historic text compares creation with the greater beauty of Jesus. Hymnary specifically discusses Psalm 27:4. Modern arrangement verses not verified.

## As the Deer
Primary: longing for god, devotion to god. Secondary: trust, spiritual thirst.
Source: https://hymnary.org/hymn/GG2013/626
Note: Hymnary identifies the Marty Nystrom text as a paraphrase of Psalm 42:1 with additional material from Psalm 28:7. Copyright-limited lyrics; this is source-backed textual commentary, not a line-by-line inspection.

Caveat: Hymnary editorial commentary and published hymn indexes may establish themes without displaying licensed modern lyrics; exact The Well arrangements still require checks.
