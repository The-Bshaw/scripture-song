# Scripture & Song — reviewed matching release

## Matching rules
- Direct biblical connection: lyric text adapts a specific biblical text, with song-specific evidence.
- Publisher Scripture association: publisher/source associates the song to the passage; NOT a claim of direct quotation.
- Strong theological connection: reviewed primary song theme overlaps a distinctive passage topic, and the song has a relevant editorial Scripture lead or multiple distinctive primary-theme overlaps.
- Related/secondary themes are stored separately and do NOT cause search hits.
- Topic searches match reviewed primary themes only.
- The Well library is ordered before additional songs.

## Integrity limits
Only three links previously possessed song-specific source support. The other 133 preexisting editorial references have not been individually corroborated. They are retained as evidence-tagged leads, NOT upgraded to verified. No theme listing from Worship Together is treated as a direct lyric-to-verse assertion. New Google Sheet songs enter the review queue.
