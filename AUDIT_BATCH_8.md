# Batch 8: published hymn text reviews

These are **source-verified for the historical texts**, not necessarily for The Well arrangement or added verses. Source pages and notes follow.

## Doxology
Primary: praise, trinity. Secondary: thanksgiving, creation.
Source: https://hymnary.org/text/praise_god_from_whom_all_blessings_ken
Note: Traditional Thomas Ken text, not a modern variant; Psalm 150:1 is an editorial thematic lead.

## What a Friend we Have in Jesus
Primary: prayer, trust in jesus. Secondary: comfort, trials.
Source: https://hymnary.org/text/what_a_friend_we_have_in_jesus_all_our_s
Note: Prayer in hardship and confidence in Christ as friend; published historic text checked.

## Come Thou Long Expected Jesus
Primary: advent, longing for christ. Secondary: deliverance, kingdom of christ.
Source: https://hymnary.org/text/come_thou_long_expected_jesus_born_to
Note: Historical Wesley verses center anticipated arrival and liberating reign of Christ; modern added verses not checked.

## Hark the Herald Angels Sing
Primary: incarnation, birth of christ. Secondary: reconciliation, new birth.
Source: https://hymnary.org/text/hark_the_herald_angels_sing_glory_to
Note: Historic text includes biblical allusions to Luke 2:14, John 1:14, Galatians 4:4; not a resurrection-primary song.

## O Come all Ye Faithful
Primary: adoration, birth of christ. Secondary: incarnation, praise.
Source: https://hymnary.org/text/o_come_all_ye_faithful_joyful_and_triump
Note: Historic text invites worship of incarnate Christ; specific recording verses unverified.

## The Wonderful Cross (When I Survey)
Primary: cross of christ, surrender. Secondary: atonement, humility.
Source: https://hymnary.org/text/when_i_survey_the_wondrous_cross_watts
Note: Verified Isaac Watts source text only; Chris Tomlin added refrain not independently checked in this pass.
