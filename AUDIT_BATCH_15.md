# Audit batch 15

Five new published-source lyric reviews, see `audit-reviews.json`.

- **well-032**: love for god, adoration. Secondary: praise, joy in worship. Source: https://www.hymnallibrary.org/hymns/i-love-you-lord/. Laurie Klein composition confirmed via Hymnary; focus is affectionate worship and offering praise. Scripture leads: Psalm 18:1; Psalm 19:14, thematic, not quotations.
- **song-0081**: holiness of god, worthiness of the lamb. Secondary: adoration, heavenly worship. Source: https://worshipleader.com/worship-song/agnus-dei/. Michael W. Smith composition; worship of the Holy Lamb. Revelation 4:8; 5:12 (thematic/close biblical-language parallels).
- **well-096**: corporate praise, spiritual awakening. Secondary: revival, unity in worship. Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/did-you-feel-the-mountains-tremble-delirious/. Delirious song from publisher lyrics. Resurrection of Christ is mentioned, but congregational awakening and public praise are the central emphases. Publisher refs Isaiah 64:3–4; Isaiah 62:10; Psalm 52:9.
- **well-110**: adoration of jesus, worthiness of christ. Secondary: incarnation, atonement, resurrection. Source: https://integrityworship.com/songs/behold-him-paul-baloche/. Paul Baloche/Mitch Wong composition according to Integrity Worship; central repeated call is to behold and adore Jesus. Publisher refs Isaiah 6:3, John 1:29, Revelation 1:7. Exact arrangement still unconfirmed.
- **well-115**: humility of christ, incarnation. Secondary: kingship of christ, atonement, resurrection. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LN1cqABGM5Q. Phil Wickham official lyric video. Main contrast: the King chooses a humble birth, yet reigns. Cross and resurrection appear as supporting gospel storyline. Exact church recording not yet confirmed.
