# Bible book and chapter summary research

Research sources for original editorial writing (do not copy their protected prose):

- BibleProject book literary overviews: https://bibleproject.com/downloads/ — book-level structure and biblical theology.
- BibleRef: https://www.bibleref.com/Book-Summaries.html — book backgrounds and chapter argument flow.
- Bible Gateway chapter overview announcement: https://www.biblegateway.com/learn/bible-gateway-updates/chapter-overviews/ — vetted chapter overviews, model for editorial review.
- Everyday Scripture: https://everydayscripture.org/summaries — every chapter, narrative detail and context.
- VerseNotes: https://versenotes.org/chapter-by-chapter/ — chapter notes with context and memorable verses.
- Bible Glide: https://www.bibleglide.com/passages — chapter themes beside Bible text.
- Bible Summary: https://www.biblesummary.org.uk/ — quick all-chapter cross-check, not sufficient for detailed summaries.

Editorial requirement: independently compare each chapter with biblical text, then write a distinctive 100-160-word overview and 3-6 chapter-specific themes; mark review status separately. 1,189 chapters require a phased editorial pass. Current package has intentionally incomplete coverage, tracked transparently in CHAPTER_COVERAGE.csv.
