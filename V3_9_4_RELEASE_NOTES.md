# Scripture & Song 3.9.4 – Compact Song Library

- Song Library: removed eyebrow, descriptive sentence, collection total and search label; reduced header height.
- Song Search: smaller heading, reduced hero height, desktop headline stays on one line where space allows.
- New centered, connected collection tabs: All Songs (default), Global, Ancient, Heritage, Advent, Shelved. Tabs filter the grid instead of scrolling to sections.
- Alphabetical song cards (left-to-right in three desktop columns) show song titles only, with the collection tag in All Songs. Artist descriptions removed.
- Spotify: clicking a round play button opens one 80px Spotify embed on demand. Opening another closes the previous iframe; clicking again collapses it. Browsers/Spotify may require a second click inside the embed to start playback. No unsupported hidden playback is attempted.
- Songs without a verified Spotify track link remain discoverable with Spotify search, without inventing recordings.
- Preserves song data, chapter data, existing matching engine, and Sheet synchronization.
