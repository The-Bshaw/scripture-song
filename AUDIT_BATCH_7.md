# Lyrical audit batch 7: SongSelect-informed

SongSelect publicly confirms broad category taxonomy but individual lyrics/themes are not consistently available without sign-in. These four reviews use accessible Worship Together song-specific lyric pages. Publisher-provided theme lists were not blindly imported.

## You've Already Won
Primary: trust in christ, victory in christ. Secondary: hope, perseverance, eternal life.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/youve-already-won-shane-shane/?nav=1
Scripture leads: Exodus 14:14, Revelation 21:1-4.
Analysis: Repeated confidence in Christ’s already-won victory under hardship. Future life with Christ and provision are supporting assurances, not primary resurrection statements. Publisher lists many broad themes; primary assignment follows lyric emphasis.

## How Deep the Father's Love for us
Primary: love of god, atonement. Secondary: humility, resurrection.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/how-deep-the-fathers-love-for-us
Scripture leads: Isaiah 53:12, Ephesians 2:4-9.
Analysis: The Father giving the Son and Christ bearing sin are central. Resurrection appears as a supporting affirmation. Publisher classification and Scripture references corroborated.

## 10,000 Reasons
Primary: praise, thankfulness. Secondary: goodness of god, faithfulness.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/10-000-reasons-bless-the-lord/?nav=1
Scripture leads: Psalm 103:1-5.
Analysis: Recurring call to praise the Lord through daily life, remembering his goodness and character. Psalm 103 is a theological/phrase parallel, not a certified quotation.

## How Great is our God
Primary: greatness of god, praise. Secondary: glory of god, trinity.
Source: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/how-great-is-our-god/?nav=1
Scripture leads: Psalm 93:1, Psalm 104:1-3.
Analysis: Repeated emphasis on divine greatness and adoration; the Trinity is mentioned but not the overarching refrain. Publisher lists adoration and God’s attributes, with cited Psalm references.

Updated songs: ["You've Already Won", "How Deep the Father's Love for us", '10,000 Reasons', 'How Great is our God']. Cumulative data source-backed: 56/117.
