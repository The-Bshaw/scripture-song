# Balanced matching update

- The source-reviewed primary/secondary tags are unchanged.
- A controlled theological vocabulary bridges short Scripture-index topics and precise lyrical annotations.
- Topic searches include close primary matches and meaningful secondary relationships, with primary ranked higher.
- Scripture search requires a song-specific source link, one distinct primary relationship, or two distinct secondary relationships.
- Broad labels such as praise and Christ do not independently qualify songs for a passage.
- Metaphorical resurrection imagery does not qualify a song for Resurrection.
- Google Sheets sync, song data, audit files, and UI assets are preserved.
