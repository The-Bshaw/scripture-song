# Scripture & Song V3.8.6 — New Testament Complete

This release completes the remaining chapters, **Hebrews through Revelation**, yielding original editorial chapter summaries for **all 1,189 chapters of the Protestant Bible**.

- Adds **47** individual chapter summaries and five theme tags per new chapter.
- Preserves nine previously written summaries in Hebrews, James, 1 Peter, and Revelation.
- Completes Hebrews (13), James (5), 1 Peter (5), 2 Peter (3), 1 John (5), 2 John (1), 3 John (1), Jude (1), and Revelation (22).
- Updates the nine book overviews, chapter coverage CSV, and embedded local-preview dataset.
- Retains website styling, song catalog, Spotify embeds, and Google Sheets sync scripts and workflows.
- Increments the browser cache-busting script version to 3.8.6.

## Verification
- 1,189 / 1,189 Bible chapters contain chapter-level editorial summaries.
- Every chapter has at least one theme entry; all newly authored chapters have exactly five.
- Local preview data was regenerated from the source JSON files.
- ZIP CRC and JavaScript syntax checked during packaging.

## Editorial note
Summaries and theme assignments are original editorial drafts and still require a human verse-by-verse theological review before being described as professionally verified. Imagery in Revelation is highly symbolic; interpretive distinctions should be handled with care.
