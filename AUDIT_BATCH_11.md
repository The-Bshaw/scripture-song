# Audit batch 11 — verified source review

Added five lyric-page reviews. Source-backed thematic classifications do not certify exact Spotify recording arrangements.

- **More Like Jesus** — primary: christlikeness, surrender; secondary: humility, discipleship; evidence: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/more-like-jesus-passion/. Repeated prayer to become like Jesus and surrender everything; crucifixion and servanthood supply the theological basis.
- **Morning by Morning** — primary: god's faithfulness, trust in god; secondary: daily mercy, surrender; evidence: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/morning-by-morning-pat-barrett/. Central repeated affirmation of daily faithfulness and trust; publisher lists both references.
- **My Worth is Not in What I Own** — primary: identity in christ, worth in christ; secondary: atonement, contentment; evidence: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/my-worth-is-not-in-what-i-own-at-the-cross-keith-and-kristyn-getty/. Lyrics contrast worldly measures of worth with the redemption of Christ; secondary references are editorial.
- **The Lord our God** — primary: god's faithfulness, trust in god; secondary: guidance, provision; evidence: https://www.worshiptogether.com/songs/the-lord-our-god-passion/. Repeated emphasis on unchanging faithfulness and keeping promises; publisher lists James 1:17.
- **Thank You Jesus for the Blood** — primary: atonement, salvation through christ; secondary: forgiveness, resurrection; evidence: https://dev.worshiptogether.com/songs/thank-you-jesus-for-the-blood-charity-gayle/?nav=1. Emphasis on Christ’s blood securing salvation; resurrection described but not the primary repeated theme. Publisher scripture references supplied.
